3D_CFI

Masamichi Zaizen
arXiv:

These animations show the flavor evolution induced by collisional neutrino-flavor instability (so-called CFI). Each panel exhibit \( \nu_e \) population, flavor coherence, and norm of the polarization vector averaged over energy, angle, and space, respectively.

Model: \( \bar{R}_0 = 10^{-5} \) case where a near (gapless or plus) mode is dominant

Model: \( \bar{R}_0 = 10^{-4} \) case where a far (gapped or minus) mode is dominant
